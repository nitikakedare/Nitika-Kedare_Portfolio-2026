ZINE DESIGN

BEHIND THE
SCREEN

Tracing Indian Shadow Puppetry

Assignment Brief:
The brief was to choose a topic rooted in the Indian context and explore it through the format of a zine, encouraging experimentation with storytelling, structure, and visual language.

My design process began by exploring the visual storytelling of traditional Indian shadow puppetry and the role of light and silhouettes in performance. The zine uses layered folds and cut forms to gradually reveal characters and scenes, echoing the unfolding experience of a shadow play. Through this interactive structure, the book becomes a tactile narrative where movement and form guide the viewer through the story.

The zine explores the tradition of storytelling before the presence of printed books and digital media, when stories travelled through memory and were passed from voice to voice across generations. It draws inspiration from the art of shadow puppetry, where light and silhouettes transform narratives into visual performances. Through this theme, the book reflects on how communities once experienced epics and folklore collectively through the interplay of light, shadow, and performance.

PUBLICATION DESIGN

FASHION AS
RESISTANCE

Tracing the garments that shaped acts of resistance

Assignment Brief:
The project involved developing an A-Z glossary publication based on a historical theme, examining how objects, practices, or symbols reflect broader cultural and social narratives. The focus was on translating research into a structured editorial format through typography, layout design, and visual storytelling.

Initial Ideation:
While exploring possible historical themes, I was drawn to how clothing often carries meaning far beyond aesthetics. This led me to investigate garments that have played a role in resistance, identity, and cultural survival.

Project Overview:
My publication, Fashion as Resistance (Tracing the Garments that Shaped Acts of Resistance), is an A-Z glossary of garments and adornments across Asia that have been tied to moments of protest, resilience, and cultural assertion. Each entry highlights how clothing such as khadi, protest sarees, or traditional ornaments became a silent yet powerful language of resistance.
