CAMPAIGN DESIGN

The design process began by exploring the brand philosophy of the phone “Nothing”, which emphasizes minimalism and clarity in both form and experience. The campaign is built around the statement “When you have NOTHING in hand, you have EVERYTHING,” highlighting how simplicity can translate into power, possibility, and vision. Typography becomes the primary visual element, creating bold impact while the product integrates within the composition to reinforce the message. The restrained black-and-white palette reflects the brand’s minimal aesthetic while maintaining strong visual contrast and focus.
