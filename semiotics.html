EAGLE x
WINDOW

Assignment Brief:

The brief was to select two objects from different categories and combine them to create a set of visual symbols that communicate meaning. Using design principles such as scale, repetition, proportion, and figure-ground relationships, 30 symbol iterations were explored and refined into a final set of 5 cohesive symbols.

Concept & Symbol Development

For this project, I combined the forms of an eagle and a window to explore ideas of vision, perspective, and ambition. The shapes were simplified and merged to create minimal symbols that communicate meaning clearly while remaining visually consistent as a set.

Poster Visualisation

The symbols were translated into a series of posters imagined for the locker rooms of a fictional school sports team.











Each poster paired a symbol with a short motivational message to reinforce themes of determination and team spirit.

Mockups & Applications

To test their versatility, the symbols were also applied to merchandise mockups such as sports apparel and accessories. These applications explore how the graphics can function beyond posters while maintaining a unified visual language.
