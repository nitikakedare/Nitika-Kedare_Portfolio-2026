MAHINDRA EXCELLENCE IN
THEATRE AWARDS

PROJECT BRIEF

The brief required designing a poster for a chosen event using typography as the main visual language. The process involved selecting an event and exploring how typographic form and composition could communicate its identity without the use of imagery.

The design process began by exploring how typography alone could capture the essence of theatre. Repeated horizontal lines were introduced to create rhythm and movement, drawing inspiration from stage curtains and theatrical lighting. A high-contrast black and white palette was used to emphasise drama and focus attention on the typographic composition.

In the second stage of the poster, colour was introduced to enhance the visual impact of the composition. The use of red adds intensity and theatrical drama, reflecting the expressive nature of stage performances. A gradient transition from bright red to darker tones creates depth while guiding attention towards the central typography.

MOTION
TEASER REEL

The motion reel was developed as a teaser for the META event, introducing the identity through a typographic narrative. Moving horizontal lines were animated to create rhythm and motion, drawing inspiration from theatre curtains and stage lighting. The event name and “2026” were gradually revealed to establish hierarchy and guide the viewer’s attention, while the slow pacing created a cinematic and dramatic mood. This exploration also marked my first experience working with Adobe After Effects.

EXPLORATIONS

As part of the exploration, an alternative colour approach was developed using yellow and orange tones transitioning into darker shades to create a glowing stage-light effect. The zig-zag structure introduces a sense of movement, reflecting the dynamic nature of theatrical performances. The gradient adds rhythm and depth while maintaining strong contrast against the dark background.
